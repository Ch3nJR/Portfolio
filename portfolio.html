About me

My name is Justin, and I'm on an unconventional path that most people wouldn't expect — from Information Technology to Psychology.

My IT background gave me something invaluable: the ability to think in systems, to find patterns in complexity, and to approach problems with methodical rigour. Now I'm channelling that foundation toward understanding the most complex system of all — the human mind.

"Technology without understanding people is just code. I want to build both halves of that equation."

Currently pursuing my degree in Psychology, I'm drawn to areas like cognitive psychology, behavioural science, and human-computer interaction — spaces where my two worlds genuinely overlap. Ideally, I want to be part of the solution where people can freely share their troubles and emotions during troubled times.

Whether in a research setting, clinical support role, or an organisation that values human-centred thinking, I bring a cross-disciplinary edge that few candidates can offer.
